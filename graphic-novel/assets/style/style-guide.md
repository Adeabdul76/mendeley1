# "Warm Pop" house style

Original style, defined only in descriptive words (CLAUDE.md §5). Never name an artist, studio or publisher here or in any prompt.

- **Line:** clean, confident ink outlines, medium weight, slightly thicker outer contours, minimal hatching.
- **Colour:** bright warm palette, flat cel shading with one soft shadow tone and a subtle rim light; gradients only on skies/backgrounds.
- **Characters:** semi-realistic cartoon proportions (~6 heads adults, 4–5 children), expressive eyes; likeness carried by hair, skin tone, glasses, facial hair, headwear, signature clothes.
- **Backgrounds:** simplified but specific, soft depth of field on busy scenes.
- **Mood:** upbeat, humorous, family-friendly; big reaction faces on comedy beats.

Prompt text lives in `apps/worker/prompts/style_block.txt` and `negative.txt`.
